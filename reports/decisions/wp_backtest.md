# Win probability: our walk-forward model vs nflfastR (G1)

Generated by `uv run twm decisions wp-backtest` (code: src/twm/modules/decisions/; walk-through: notebooks/03_wp_model.ipynb). Win probability (WP) = the chance that the team with the ball wins, from the situation before the snap. Lower Brier and log loss = better forecasts; the calibration error says how far, on average, '70%' forecasts are from winning 70% of the time.

## Setup

- **Rows**: one per scrimmage play with a valid pre-snap state, from the warehouse's `fact_play` + `fact_game` (1999-2025). Dropped: 267,183 rows that are not a scrimmage play (kickoffs, extra points, two-point tries, timeouts, `no_play` penalty rows whose down is replayed), 19 with a missing or impossible state, 2,600 plays of the 15 tie games (the label 'the team with the ball wins' does not apply; excluded from training and metrics).
- **Label**: the possession team before the snap won the game.
- **Walk-forward**: 20 folds, test seasons 2006-2025. The first fold is 'test 2006: train 1999-2005, tune on 1999-2004 -> validate on 2005'; the last is 'test 2025: train 1999-2024, tune on 1999-2023 -> validate on 2024'. Each model learns from earlier seasons only; its settings are tuned on the season before the test season (early stopping, 4 settings: 31 or 63 leaves x at least 200 or 1,000 plays per leaf, learning rate 0.05, log loss), then it is refit on every earlier season. LightGBM, single-threaded and deterministic.
- **Features**: score difference, seconds left in the game and in the half, half, down, distance, yards to the end zone, both teams' timeouts, receives the second-half kickoff (from the opening kickoff), home / away / neutral, the closing spread from the offense's view, era flags (2015 extra-point rule, 2023+ kickoff rules) and two time interactions (spread x time left, lead / time left: nflfastR's own, chosen on 1999-2003 -> 2004-2005 validation before any test season was scored). Monotone: a bigger lead or spread, more own timeouts, fewer yards to go or to the end zone, an earlier down and getting the ball after halftime never lower the offense's WP; more defensive timeouts never raise it.
- **Calibration**: isotonic only if it beats the raw model out of sample on the validation season (fit on half its games, scored on the other half). Kept in 0 of 20 folds.
- **Comparison**: 753,923 of 753,925 test plays (5,417 games) have nflfastR's `wp` and `vegas_wp`; every number below is on those plays. Intervals: 95%, season-block bootstrap (2,000 resamples). The calibration error's interval is rough: resampling adds noise to every bin, which pushes the error up, so its interval can sit at or above the point value.
- **Caveat** (PROJECT_SPEC 6.3): nflfastR's models were fit once on many seasons, including seasons tested here, so their numbers are partly in-sample; ours never saw the season it scores. `vegas_wp` uses the same closing spread as ours; `wp` uses none.

A season flag cannot tell the rule change from anything else that changed in the same season (more passing, more fourth-down attempts), so read it as 'the league since 2015', not as the value of the extra-point rule.

## Feature importance (smoke test)

Gain shares in the 2025 model: score_differential 61%, diff_time_ratio 13%, spread_time 12%, posteam_spread 11%, yardline_100 2%, game_seconds_remaining 0%. A feature above 40% is flagged by the harness: 20 of 20 folds (score_differential). Expected for win probability: the score is the state's main fact, and it is known before the snap (no leakage).
