## xFP check (warehouse)

xFP is re-scored from ffopportunity's expected components with config/scoring.yaml. With the nflverse-PPR weights (ffopportunity's own) it should reproduce `total_fantasy_points_exp` up to ffopportunity's rounding: each expected component and each part total is stored with 2 decimals, so up to 0.1412 points.

| player-games | equal to the cent | within rounding | beyond rounding | of which rushing 2-pt tries | max difference | max without rushing 2-pt |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 106,560 | 15,197 (14.3%) | 106,463 (99.9%) | 97 | 97 | 0.51 | 0.079 |

The rows beyond rounding are rushing two-point tries: ffopportunity's weekly `rush_fantasy_points_exp` adds 0.1 x the expected yards of a two-point try, which its `rush_yards_gained_exp` (and real scoring) leaves out (docs/waiver_radar.md).
