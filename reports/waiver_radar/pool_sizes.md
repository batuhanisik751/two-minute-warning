# Waiver Radar candidate pool: sizes and validation

Generated by `uv run twm radar pool-report` from the warehouse built at 2026-09-28 02:21:27 (UTC). Deterministic: rerun on the same warehouse to get the same file. Rules and definitions: `docs/waiver_radar.md`.

A player is **in the pool** (probably on waivers in a 12-team league) when he is on an NFL roster (status ACT, INA, DEV) and outside the top N at his position by BOTH the preseason list and points per game so far. N = QB 18, RB 36, WR 36, TE 18 (config/league.yaml: starter threshold x 1.5). Preseason list: FantasyPros' preseason cheat sheet (method `ecr`) where one exists, else last season's PPG rank (min 4 games) with rookies drafted in rounds 1-2 counted as drafted (method `prior_ppg`).

One as-of per regular-season week (the Tuesday 14:00 UTC after it), for every week whose games are all in the cache (the current season stops at the last complete week).
