## How to read it

- **Chance**: how often players the Radar rated like him became a fantasy starter (finished a week in the top 12 QBs, top 24 RBs, top 24 WRs or top 12 TEs) within their team's next 3 games, in the 2014-2025 backtest (91,638 predictions, each made by a model that had not seen that season). In brackets, the 90% range of that rate: "similar players hit 45-55%". The **model** column is the Radar's own probability; it runs high for the top players, so trust the chance.
- **Priority** (top 25 only): **must-add** when similar players hit at least 50% of the time, **speculative** from 25% to 50%, **watch** below. How each tier really did is in the table below.
- **Why**: the facts that raised his chance the most (the model's own weights), true as of the Tuesday; at most one per topic (playing time, targets, carries ...).
- **When**: after Monday night's game and Tuesday's data updates (the as-of is Tuesday 14:00 UTC), before your league's waivers clear (often Wednesday; check your league).
- A chance is not a promise: in 2014-2025, 47.8% of the Radar's top-10 picks became starters (3,501 of 7,320); the rest did not.

## About this list

- **Model** `logit-5828a082c9e09093`: logistic regression for `y_hit` (at least one starter week in the next 3 games), trained on 2013-2025 (96,220 player-weeks, 10,371 hits), settings tuned and probabilities calibrated on 2025; the walk-forward fold for test season 2026 (never trained on 2026).
- **Pool**: players on an NFL roster at the as-of, outside the top N at their position by both a preseason ranking and points per game (docs/waiver_radar.md).
- **Chance and priority** from the stored logit backtest of 2014-2025 (bins of at least 500 predictions, 38 bins; docs/waiver_radar.md "Weekly list").
- **Reasons**: each feature's contribution to the model's log-odds (its weight times how far the player's value is from the training average); the largest positive ones, one per topic, phrased from the registry (docs/glossary.md). Features phrased with a generic sentence: none.
- Unofficial and educational, not affiliated with the NFL or ESPN. Predictions are probabilistic and frequently wrong. Not betting advice. Data: nflverse; rankings: FantasyPros via DynastyProcess; snap counts: Pro Football Reference.
